Tamer tips

  • Babies fall asleep after a while. Walk up to one and press Space to try taming it.
  • Wolves, rabbits and bears are easy to tame. Rare animals need the Appletor or Slingshot to raise your odds.
  • The whole map changes biome every few minutes. Each season has its own animals and a boss.
  • Gold is your score. Windmills earn it while you are away.
